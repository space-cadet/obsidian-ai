**Grammar Migration Fixture Results**:

| MsgCap | Mode | ToolTok | Total | Peak | vs Unlimited |

**Key Findings**:
1. `maxContextMessages: 10` is the dominant factor — cuts tokens by ~42%
2. `maxToolResultTokens` difference (64000 vs 4000) is only ~11% in preserve mode
3. `elide` mode is extremely aggressive (6.8K vs 85K tokens) — confirms T62a severity
4. Attachment fixture shows same pattern but less dramatic (fewer total turns)
