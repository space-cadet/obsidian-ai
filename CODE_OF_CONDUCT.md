# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in Obsidian AI welcoming and respectful for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or technical preference.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind and constructive.
- Assume good intent, and ask clarifying questions when something is unclear.
- Respect different workflows and accessibility needs.
- Keep discussions focused on improving the project.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeatedly derailing issues, pull requests, or discussions.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or block participants who violate this code of conduct. Serious concerns can be reported privately to the project maintainer through GitHub.

This code of conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant.
